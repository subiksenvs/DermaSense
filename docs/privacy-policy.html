Overview & Medical Disclaimer

Welcome to DermaSense ("we," "our," or "us"). We build AI-driven skin wellness and tracking technologies to help individuals better understand their skin conditions, maintain personalized skincare routines, and connect with healthcare professionals.

This Privacy Policy applies to the DermaSense Mobile Application (Application ID: com.dermasense.app) and related API endpoints.

Important Medical Disclaimer: DermaSense is intended for educational, cosmetic skin wellness, and informational tracking purposes only. It is NOT a certified medical diagnostic device and does not diagnose, cure, mitigate, treat, or prevent any disease or skin condition. Always seek the advice of a board-certified dermatologist or qualified healthcare provider with any medical questions.

2

Information We Collect

To provide high-accuracy visual analysis, routine tracking, and localized clinic suggestions, we collect the following categories of information:

Category Data Elements Purpose
Account & Identity Name, Email Address, Account Password Hash, User ID Authentication, profile management, and secure account synchronization.
Skin Photos & Scans User-captured photos, skin metric heatmaps, analysis timestamps AI evaluation of hydration, pores, redness, texture, eye bags, firmness, and radiance.
Location Data Approximate or precise GPS coordinates (only when requested) Finding and displaying nearby dermatologists and medical facilities.
Technical & Diagnostics Device model, OS version, crash reports, performance metrics Ensuring app stability, fixing bugs, and optimizing inference speed.

Camera & Gallery Access: We request camera and photo storage permissions only when you take a photo for skin analysis or upload a profile picture. Photos are processed exclusively for your wellness reports.

3

How We Use Your Information

We process collected data solely to deliver, enhance, and secure your experience:

  • AI Model Inference: Processing skin scans through computer vision pipelines to generate visual metric maps.
  • Personalized Skincare Routines: Formulating customized AM/PM skincare habits based on your skin profile.
  • Historical Tracking: Enabling you to compare historical scan results and track changes over weeks and months.
  • Doctor & Clinic Discovery: Locating certified skincare specialists and clinics near your current position.
  • Service Reliability: Identifying software exceptions and maintaining system uptime.

No Commercial Data Sharing: We do not sell your personal information or biometric scans to third-party advertisers, pharmaceutical companies, or data brokers.

4

Third-Party Services & Infrastructure

DermaSense integrates trusted cloud infrastructure partners that adhere to stringent data protection standards:

  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud Platform: Provides cloud authentication, real-time database management, and encrypted storage.
  • OpenStreetMap: Delivers interactive map tiles and geographic point-of-interest data for locating doctors.
  • Private AI Inference Cluster: Proprietary backend endpoints hosted on secure cloud infrastructure utilizing TLS 1.3 encryption.
5

Data Security & Storage

We maintain multi-layered administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to defend your personal data:

  • Encryption in Transit: 100% of network traffic between your mobile device and our backend servers is encrypted using modern HTTPS and TLS 1.3 protocol.
  • Encrypted Storage: User records and uploaded scan artifacts are securely encrypted at rest.
  • Strict Access Controls: Only authenticated services with least-privilege access keys can access data clusters.
6

Data Retention & Right to Erasure

You have full ownership of your data at all times. We retain your profile information and scan history only for as long as your account remains active.

How to Delete Your Data:

  • In-App Deletion: Go to Profile ➔ Account Settings ➔ Delete Account. This action immediately purges your profile, scan history, and photos from our active databases.
  • Email Request: You can send an account deletion request to subiksen.v.s@gmail.com with the subject "Data Erasure Request". We will process your request within 7 business days.
7

Children's Privacy

DermaSense is not directed towards children under the age of 13 (or 16 in certain jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from minors. If you believe that a minor has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us immediately so we can delete the information.
