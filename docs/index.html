Privacy Policy

DermaSense - AI Skin Health & Wellness Application

Last Updated: October 5, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to DermaSense ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about how your personal information and health-related data are handled. This Privacy Policy explains how our application (Package ID: com.dermasense.app) collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use our mobile application and related services.

Medical Disclaimer: DermaSense is designed for educational, cosmetic skin wellness, and informational tracking purposes only. It is NOT a medical diagnostic device and does not substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or clinical treatment.

2. Information We Collect

To provide skin analysis, personalized routines, and nearby doctor discovery, we collect the following types of information:

A. Account & Profile Information

  • Personal Identifiers: Name, email address, and profile settings created during authentication.
  • Skin Profile: Self-reported skin type, sensitivity levels, age group, and skincare goals.

B. Skin Images & AI Scan Data

  • Photographs: Images of skin concerns captured or uploaded through your camera or photo gallery for AI analysis.
  • Analysis Metrics: AI-generated health metrics including skin hydration, texture quality, redness, pore dilation, eye bags, and firmness indicators.

C. Location Information

  • Approximate / Precise Location: Collected only with your explicit permission to help you find nearby verified dermatologists, skincare clinics, and medical centers. Location data is never used to track your background movements.

D. Device and Usage Data

  • Technical Information: Device model, operating system version, crash reports, and app performance analytics to ensure stability.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process your data for the following essential purposes:

  • To perform visual skin analysis and generate instant wellness insights.
  • To recommend customized daily morning and evening skincare regimens.
  • To enable visual tracking of skin changes and improvement over time.
  • To locate and display healthcare specialists and clinics near your current location.
  • To maintain account security and provide customer support.

Our Promise: We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data, skin photographs, or health metrics to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

4. Third-Party Services & Infrastructure

DermaSense uses industry-standard third-party providers for secure infrastructure:

  • Google Firebase: Used for secure user authentication, database management, and cloud storage.
  • OpenStreetMap: Used to render maps and specialist clinic locations.
  • Secure Cloud Inference: Private endpoints with TLS encryption to securely process AI analysis requests.

5. Data Security & Storage

We apply robust security measures to protect your sensitive data:

  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted between the mobile app and our servers is secured using modern HTTPS/TLS 1.3 encryption.
  • Encrypted Storage: Account credentials and scan logs are stored in secure, access-controlled cloud environments.

6. Data Retention & Your Rights

You have full control over your personal data:

  • Access and Export: You may request a copy of your stored skin analysis logs.
  • Account & Data Deletion: You can delete your account and associated scan history at any time directly in the app profile settings or by sending an email request to our support team.

7. Children's Privacy

DermaSense is not intended for use by children under the age of 13 (or 16 depending on jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
